# Code of Conduct

KIAS is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically rigorous community.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate with patience, empathy, and professional respect;
- critique ideas and evidence rather than people;
- welcome contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience;
- give clear, actionable feedback;
- protect private, personal, security-sensitive, and confidential information;
- acknowledge mistakes and help repair their impact;
- prioritize the safety and long-term health of the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention;
- insults, personal attacks, deliberate humiliation, or sustained disruption;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- exposing credentials, personal data, employer/customer identities, internal documents, or security reports;
- using project infrastructure to facilitate abuse or unauthorized access;
- bad-faith manipulation of reviews, benchmarks, vulnerabilities, or contributor credit.

## Scope

This policy applies to repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, community calls, project communication channels, and public representation of the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary conduct concerns, contact the maintainers through a private channel identified in `MAINTAINERS.md`. Security or privacy incidents must use the private process in `SECURITY.md`.

Maintainers may remove content, issue warnings, limit participation, temporarily suspend access, or permanently ban participants when necessary. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid unnecessary disclosure of personal information.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by widely used open-source community conduct frameworks while being written specifically for KIAS.
